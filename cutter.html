Printing tips

  • Print lip down, blade up, no supports. It's modelled that way (and mirrored to match), so flip it over to cut.
  • 0.2 mm layers or finer. The 0.4 mm edge is a single wall with a 0.4 mm nozzle: turn on thin-wall detection ("Detect thin walls" / "Print thin walls") so the slicer keeps it.
  • The side text is separate raised letters that overlap the blade; slicers (Prusa, Bambu, Orca, Cura) merge them automatically.
  • PETG stays stiffer in warm hands; PLA is fine for cool dough. Hand-wash only — dishwashers warp both.
  • Not certified food safe: layer lines can trap dough. Use a food-safe filament, chill the dough, and wash it right after use.
